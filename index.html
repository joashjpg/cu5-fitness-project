My advice

    Built from Fitbit data for 30 people in 2016. The chance comes from a random forest that sees only the time of day and your steps so far, and the advice assumes about 100 steps a minute, so 10 minutes of walking is about 1,000 steps. The percentages come from asking the model what would happen with that many extra steps. It doesn't know your timetable. A plan improves your odds. It can't guarantee them.
